## ⬡ Who we are

We're developers and graphic designers, working out of Mendrisio, in the Italian-speaking south of Switzerland. Design and engineering sit in the same room, on the same project, from the first sketch to production.

We've written more than **one million lines of code** over the years. That number isn't a trophy. It's the reason we can tell, early, which ideas will hold up and which ones will quietly rot in six months.

Hexagon exists because of a false choice we kept watching companies make:

- **Buy off-the-shelf** and bend the business around software that was never built for it.
- **Customise something generic** until it works, and lose the identity, the speed and half the budget in the process.

Neither is acceptable. We build software that fits the business it belongs to — tailored technology, real functionality, and design that isn't an afterthought. Technology isn't the point. Simplifying, optimising and improving how people actually work is.

We're **Swiss Made Software** certified: Swiss quality, reliability and security standards, and we're held to them.

## ⬡ How we work with you

- **Design and engineering from day one.** Not a design phase thrown over a wall to developers. The people who draw it and the people who build it are on the project together, from the first sketch.
- **Tailored, not adapted.** We don't take a generic product and bend it until it almost fits. We build what the business actually does — and we say so when a standard tool would serve you better.
- **You talk to the people building it.** Small team, direct line. No account manager relaying your question to someone you never meet.
- **We're there after launch.** Shipping is the middle of the project, not the end. Maintenance, updates and evolution are part of the work.
- **Your data stays yours.** Swiss Made Software standards, and client data handled with the discretion the certification implies. Confidentiality isn't a clause we negotiate.
